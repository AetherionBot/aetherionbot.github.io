PremiumManage Server

Premium: set Aetherion's name, personality and avatar here. Fill in nothing to see them. Only the options you fill in change. Reset asks you to confirm first and can’t be combined with the other options. The name also works as a wake word, like saying “Aetherion”, and needs the Change Nickname permission. Viewing and reset work on any server; changing needs Premium.

  • name?New name, 2-32 characters, no mentions or links (also works as a wake word)
  • personality?Tone, style or character, max 500 characters, e.g. 'a cheerful pirate who loves puns'
  • avatar?Profile picture for this server (PNG/JPG/GIF, max 8 MB)
  • reset?Put the name, personality and avatar back to default (asks you to confirm). Use it on its own.
