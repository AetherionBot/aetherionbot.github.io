Setup

Why does Aetherion ask for so many permissions?

The invite link asks for one permission bundle (309576723664). It does not include Administrator, Kick, Ban or Manage Server. Each permission is tied to a feature:

PermissionWhy Aetherion asks for it
View Channels, Send MessagesRead the channels you add it to and reply in them. Nothing works without these.
Send Messages in Threads, Create Public ThreadsReply inside threads, and open a thread when you ask Aetherion to.
Embed LinksPost the gold embeds used by /help, games, the wallet and the now-playing panel.
Attach FilesSend images, video and audio it generates, welcome banners and the drawn game tables.
Read Message HistoryFollow a conversation and reply chain, and let /purge see the messages it deletes.
Add ReactionsAdd the reactions for reaction-role and color-role panels, which you set up in the web dashboard.
Use External EmojisUse your server's custom emojis in its replies and panels.
Manage RolesGive out roles for reaction roles, color roles and /setup autorole. Discord also needs its role to sit above them (see below).
Manage ChannelsRename the voice channel for /setup datechannel each night.
View Audit LogShow who did what in /setup logs entries (for example who deleted a message or changed a role).
Manage MessagesNeeded for /purge and message cleanup.
Change NicknameLets the Customize page in the web dashboard rename Aetherion in your server.
Connect, SpeakJoin a voice channel and talk or play audio: /play music, /voice, /audio and Read aloud.

You can switch individual permissions off in Server Settings → Roles. Everything else keeps working; only the feature in the table stops.

Why can't Aetherion give out a role? How do I move its role up?

Discord only lets a bot hand out roles that sit below its own role. If reaction roles, color roles or auto-role fail:

  1. Open Server Settings → Roles.
  2. Drag Aetherion's role above every role it should give out.
  3. Save, then try again. Make sure it still has Manage Roles.
Where did /reactionrole and /customize go? What is the web dashboard?

Reaction roles and Customize moved from slash commands to the web dashboard (beta), together with pages for giveaways, polls and AI channels (where the AI chat answers). Log in with Discord at aetherion-production-b46c.up.railway.app, pick your server, then use Roles (post, edit or delete reaction-role panels, or add the 12 color roles) or Customize (a custom name, personality and avatar; needs Premium).

You only see servers where you have Manage Server or Administrator and Aetherion is present. Reaction roles, giveaways and polls need Administrator; Settings and Customize need Manage Server. Panels you already posted keep working exactly as before. /help in Discord links to the dashboard too.

Can I choose which channels Aetherion answers in?

Yes. In the web dashboard (beta), open your server, then Features and the AI channels card (it is also linked from Settings). You need Manage Server. Pick one of three modes: everywhere (the default, so nothing changes until you choose), only in the channels you tick, or everywhere except the channels you tick. Threads and forum posts follow the channel they belong to.

Where Aetherion is told to stay quiet it does not reply, does not call the AI and does not use up anyone's daily limit. Music, games, giveaways, polls and the other commands still work in every channel, and direct messages are unchanged. There is no slash command for this yet; it lives in the dashboard.

Can one channel have its own tone, or only answer when mentioned?

Yes. On the same AI channels page, add a channel note to any text channel. The tone hint is a short line (up to 200 characters, for example friendly and brief) that Aetherion follows in that channel only. It can only describe a tone: links, mentions and anything that tries to give the AI new orders are refused. You can also tick only answer when @mentioned or replied to, so the bot ignores its name in normal chatter there but still answers a real mention or a reply.

A server can have up to 25 channel notes, and each list holds up to 100 channels. Saving rules does not need Premium, but the AI chat itself still does.

How do giveaways and polls work?

Anyone with Manage Server can run /giveaway start (prize, duration, winners, optional required role) or /poll create (a question with 2 to 10 options, an optional duration, and an option to let people pick several). Members press a button: Enter or Leave for a giveaway, one button per option for a poll.

Winners are drawn at random from people who are still in the server, and a reroll never picks the same person twice. Poll votes can be changed and are never shown by name. Each server can have up to 10 giveaways and 10 polls running. They are free, use no AI and never count toward the AI limits, and their buttons and timers keep working after a restart. The same tools are in the web dashboard (beta).

Premium

How do I redeem a Premium code?

After you pay and message Ori you get a code that looks like AETH-XXXX-XXXX. Someone with Manage Server runs this in the server that should get Premium:

/premium redeem AETH-XXXX-XXXX

Premium is active right away. Check it any time with /premium status. After 5 wrong codes in 15 minutes you are locked out for the rest of that window, so copy the code exactly.

What happens when Premium ends?

AI features switch off: AI chat, voice-note replies, live voice, /audio, Read aloud, AI welcome banners and emoji descriptions. Aetherion posts a notice when it ends.

Your custom name, personality and avatar from the web dashboard are paused, not deleted, and they come back if you renew. Everything free keeps working: music, the 7 games and Aether Coins, giveaways and polls, reaction and color roles, logs, autorole, welcome messages, /profile, /server and /top.

Nothing renews by itself. See renewals and expiry.

How do renewals work, and will I get a reminder?

One code is 30 days. Codes stack: redeem another while Premium is active and 30 days are added on top of your current end date, so renewing early loses nothing. About 3 days before Premium ends, Aetherion posts one reminder in your server's system channel (or DMs whoever redeemed the code, then the server owner, if it can't).

How do the AI, image and video limits work?

To keep things fair, each person gets 20 AI replies per day in a server, and each Premium server gets 500 AI replies per day in total. An AI reply is one answer from the AI: a chat reply, a voice-note reply or a live-voice reply. Both counts reset at midnight Eastern Time.

When you use up your own 20, or the server uses up its 500, Aetherion says which limit was reached and when it resets (at most once an hour per channel) and stays quiet until then; other people can keep going if only your own limit was reached. Music, games, roles and every other non-AI feature keep working. Images and videos have their own separate monthly limits.

Images are limited too: each person gets 1 image per month (generation or edit) in a server, and each server gets 30 images and 10 videos per month in total. They reset on the 1st at midnight Eastern Time. Errors and content-policy blocks don't count. When a server uses one up, Aetherion says so in one line, with the reset date, at most once an hour per channel.

Privacy

Does Aetherion store my messages?

Only briefly. To follow a conversation it keeps a short record of recent channel messages (the first 280 characters, up to 150 per channel), and each one expires after 3 days. If you delete a message on Discord, its saved copy is deleted too.

Voice notes and audio files are only transcribed when they are sent to the bot (a reply to it, an @mention, or its name in the text). It does not record or save voice audio. What you send to the AI (messages, images and voice clips it is asked to answer) is processed by a third-party AI provider, xAI (the company behind Grok), to write the reply. Aether Coin wallets, stats and server settings are kept until deleted. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
