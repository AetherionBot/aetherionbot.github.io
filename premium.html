What happens next

Renewals & expiry

No surprises

Codes stack

Each code is 30 days. Redeem another while Premium is active and the 30 days are added to your current end date, so renewing early loses nothing.

A reminder 3 days before

About 3 days before Premium ends, Aetherion posts one reminder in your server's system channel. If it can't, it messages whoever redeemed the code, then the server owner.

When it ends

  • AI chat, voice and audio, AI welcome banners and emoji descriptions switch off.
  • Your custom name and personality pause and come back if you renew.
  • Everything free keeps working.

Nothing renews by itself

There is no subscription and no stored payment details. To continue, pay again and redeem a new code. More in the FAQ.
