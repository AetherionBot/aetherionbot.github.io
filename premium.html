How to get Premium

Three steps

  1. 1

    Pay with PayPal

    Send $4.99 through PayPal.me. Put your Discord username and the server name in the note.

  2. 2

    Message Ori

    Tell me you paid on Discord. I'll reply with a one-time code like AETH-XXXX-XXXX. Stuck? Ask in our support server.

  3. 3

    Redeem it

    Someone with Manage Server runs /premium redeem with the code. Premium is active straight away.

Renewals & expiry

No surprises

Codes stack

Each code is 30 days. Redeem another while Premium is active and the days are added to your end date, so renewing early loses nothing.

A reminder first

About 3 days before Premium ends, Aetherion posts one reminder in your server's system channel. Check any time with /premium status.

When it ends

AI features switch off, and your custom name and personality pause until you renew. Everything free keeps working. There is no subscription and no stored payment details. More in the FAQ.
