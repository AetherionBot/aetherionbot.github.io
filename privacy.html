Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

This policy explains what the Aetherion Discord bot (“Aetherion”, “the bot”) collects, why, where it is kept, who it is shared with, and how to get it deleted. Aetherion is run by Ori, an individual developer (“I”, “me”). You can reach me through my Discord profile. Also see the Terms of Service.

The short version

  • Aetherion reads messages in the channels it can see so it can reply and follow the conversation. It keeps the most recent 150 messages per channel (the first 280 characters of each) for up to 3 days, then deletes them. If you delete a message on Discord, Aetherion deletes its saved copy too.
  • When you talk to Aetherion, send it a voice message, or speak to it in a voice channel, your words, images and audio are sent to xAI (the company behind Grok) to produce the answer.
  • Aether Coin wallets, activity stats (message counts, voice time, XP) and server settings are kept until they are deleted.
  • Aetherion does not record or save voice audio.
  • The bot never asks for or stores payment details. Premium is paid through PayPal, which shares your name, email, the amount and your note with me. The bot only keeps a small Premium record for each server.
  • I don’t sell your data, use it for advertising, or train AI models on it.
  • There is no self-serve delete command yet. Message me on Discord and I will delete your data.

1. What Aetherion collects

Aetherion only works inside Discord servers. It does not ask for passwords, email addresses or payment details, and it has no login. Premium is paid for outside the bot, through PayPal (see Premium payments).

Messages in channels it can see

Aetherion uses Discord’s message content access. In servers where it is active, for each message posted in a channel it can read (whether or not the message is for Aetherion) it keeps a short record: your Discord user ID and display name, the first 280 characters of the text, links in the message, links to attached or embedded images, and the time. It keeps its own replies the same way. This short-term history lets it follow a conversation and answer questions like “what were we just talking about?”. Each record is deleted after 3 days (sooner if the channel gets more than 150 newer messages). If a message is deleted on Discord, its saved copy is deleted too, and if it is edited, the saved copy is updated. When asked about recent conversation, it may also save a short summary of the channel (up to 600 characters).

When you talk to Aetherion

When you mention Aetherion, reply to it, or address it by name, it gathers what it needs to answer: your message and display name, images you attach or point to, the text of small text-file attachments, the message you are replying to and earlier messages in that reply chain (which may be written by other people), and a few recent lines between you and the bot in that channel. This is sent to xAI to generate the reply (see Third parties).

Voice messages and audio files

Aetherion only transcribes a Discord voice message or audio file when it is sent to the bot: a reply to one of Aetherion’s messages, a message that @mentions Aetherion (or its role), or one that uses its name in the text. It then downloads the clip, sends it to xAI to be transcribed, and posts a reply that quotes the transcript, with a spoken version. Other voice messages and audio files are not downloaded, transcribed or sent to xAI; the short channel history only notes that a voice message was posted (“[voice message]”, plus any text you typed with it). The clip itself is not saved by Aetherion.

Voice channels

When someone runs /voice join or /play, Aetherion joins their voice channel and listens only to the person who ran the command; other people’s audio is ignored. That person’s speech is cut into short clips (a few seconds each), and each clip is sent to xAI for transcription before Aetherion checks for its name; it only answers when the transcript contains its name. Silence, very short sounds (such as a cough or a one-word “ok”) and near-silent noise are skipped on the bot’s side and not sent. Audio is not recorded or saved to disk. The last 10 voice exchanges with each person are kept in memory for follow-up questions and are cleared whenever the bot restarts.

Image, video and speech generation

When you ask for an image, video, image edit, or speech (/audio or “Read aloud”), your prompt or text and any reference images are sent to xAI. Reference images can include profile pictures of members you ask about and the server’s custom emoji. The result is posted in the channel. Aetherion does not keep a library of generated media; temporary audio files used for voice playback can remain on the server until it restarts. To enforce the daily generation limit, it counts how many generations each server has used today.

Aether Coins and games

For each player: your user ID, coin balance, any bet in progress, the date you last claimed /coins daily, and when your wallet was created. Game rounds themselves are held in memory only.

Activity, levels and ranks

For each member in each server: how many messages you have sent (in total and per channel), how long you have spent in voice channels, and your XP and level, plus the date tracking began for that server. This powers /profile and /top. Message content is not part of these stats.

Server settings

Settings that server administrators choose (welcome channel, auto-role, reaction roles, date channel, and log channel and events), plus a list of the servers Aetherion is in (ID, name and member count) for my status view. Aetherion also counts how often each custom server emoji is used and stores a short AI-written description of it.

Welcome banners

If a server turns on welcome banners, a new member’s profile picture is sent to xAI to describe it and create a matching background, and their display name is drawn on the banner. The banner is posted in the server’s welcome channel and not stored by Aetherion.

Server logs

If administrators turn on /setup logs, Aetherion posts events to the channel they pick: joins and leaves, kicks, bans, timeouts, nickname, role and avatar changes, voice channel joins, moves and mute changes, posted invite links, message edits and deletions (including the earlier text when Discord still has it), and channel, role, emoji and server changes. These entries live in that Discord channel. Aetherion only stores the log settings.

Service logs

Like most software, the bot writes operational logs for debugging. These can include user and server IDs, display names, voice transcripts and spoken replies (the first 200 characters), music search terms, short excerpts of image prompts that xAI rejected, and error details. In voice channels, transcripts of speech that did not include the bot’s name can also appear in these logs.

2. How it is used

  • To run Aetherion’s features: replies, vision, voice, music, media generation, games, profiles, ranks and server tools.
  • To run Premium: activating codes, sending renewal reminders and switching AI features off when Premium ends.
  • To enforce rate limits and daily generation limits and to keep games fair.
  • To find and fix bugs and keep the service running.

Your data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or used by me to train AI models.

3. Where it is stored

Aetherion runs on Railway, a hosting provider, on servers in the United States. Stored data (message history, wallets, activity stats, settings and Premium records) sits on the bot’s persistent storage there, and service logs are kept by Railway. If you use Aetherion from outside the United States, your data is processed in the United States.

4. Third parties

  • Discord. Everything you do with Aetherion happens on Discord, which handles your data under the Discord Privacy Policy.
  • xAI (Grok). Message text, images, text-file contents, voice audio, prompts and profile pictures described above are sent to xAI’s API to create replies, transcriptions, speech, images and videos. Grok may also run web and X searches to answer. xAI processes and may retain this data under its own terms and the xAI Privacy Policy.
  • SoundCloud. When you use /play or ask for a song by voice, the song name or link is looked up on SoundCloud and the audio is streamed from there. Your identity is not sent. See the SoundCloud Privacy Policy.
  • Open Trivia Database. /trivia fetches questions from opentdb.com. No user data is sent.
  • Railway. Hosts the bot and its storage (see above).
  • PayPal. Handles Premium payments (see Premium payments). The bot itself sends nothing to PayPal.

Outside these services, data is only shared if the law requires it.

5. Premium payments

Aetherion itself never asks for or stores payment details. Premium is paid for outside the bot, through PayPal.

  • When you pay. PayPal handles the payment. As the person being paid, I receive the details PayPal shares with payees, such as your name, email address, the amount, and any note you add. PayPal handles your payment information under the PayPal Privacy Statement.
  • Getting your code. To send you a redeem code, I see your Discord username and what you write in the DM you send me.
  • What the bot stores. For each Premium server: the server ID, how Premium was granted (for example, a redeemed code), its status and expiry date, and, for redeemed codes, the user ID of the person who redeemed it and when. It also notes whether the renewal reminder and end notice were sent.

This record is used only to run Premium: activating codes, sending the reminder 3 days before Premium ends and the notice when it ends (in the server’s system channel, or by DM to the person who last redeemed a code or to the server owner), and switching AI features off at expiry.

7. Your choices and deletion

There is no self-serve delete command yet. To see or delete your data, message me through my Discord profile with your Discord user ID and what you would like removed (for example, your messages in the bot’s history, your coin wallet, or your activity stats). I will delete it and let you know when it is done. Server administrators can ask me to remove all of a server’s data the same way, including its Premium record (deleting it while Premium is active ends Premium for that server).

You can also limit what Aetherion sees:

  • Don’t mention Aetherion, reply to it or use its name in messages, voice messages or audio files you don’t want it to process.
  • Don’t use /voice join or /play if you don’t want your speech transcribed.
  • Server administrators can use Discord permissions to keep Aetherion out of specific channels, or remove it from the server.

Data held by Discord, xAI or PayPal has to be requested from them directly.

8. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (older in some countries), and Aetherion is meant for people who meet Discord’s minimum age. It is not directed at children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has used Aetherion, contact me and I will delete their data.

9. Security

Aetherion’s data is kept on private hosting that only I can access, and secrets like API keys are kept out of the data store. No system is perfectly secure, so please don’t share sensitive personal information (such as passwords, financial details or health information) with the bot.

10. This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so GitHub and Google receive standard request information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

11. Changes to this policy

If Aetherion’s features or data practices change, I will update this page and the effective date above. If you keep using Aetherion after a change, the updated policy applies.
